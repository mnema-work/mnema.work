# Where the logos of the hosts come from

The files in this folder are the vendors' own, as published, untouched: not recoloured, not resized in the file, not
redrawn. A host is shown by its logo on the home page only when its vendor's published guidelines say a third party may use
it to say that its work goes with theirs, and only for a file listed here. Every other host is shown by its name. A host
that is not in the table below is shown by its name, whatever the README says of it.

## OpenCode

The rule, from https://opencode.ai/brand (Integrations): "Plugins, providers and tools that work with OpenCode, and projects
built with it, can say so. Use a badge or a partner lockup, and name your project so it is clear that it is yours." and "Put
a badge in your README, docs or website and link it to opencode.ai. Pick the light or dark file for your background (the
HTML switches by itself), and do not change the colors or the text."

The same page says: "OpenCode and the OpenCode logo are trademarks of Anomaly. Do not use them to suggest that Anomaly
endorses or sponsors you, in your product or company name, or on merchandise without permission."

What this site does: the "Works with" badge, in its dark file (the page is dark), at its own size and colours, linked to
https://opencode.ai, beside the name of the host, which is the page's own text and not part of the badge. Nothing of
OpenCode's is recoloured, shadowed or put in another shape.

## Copilot CLI

The rule, from https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo (Usage): "Use a permitted GitHub logo to inform others that your
project integrates with GitHub." and "Use the permitted GitHub logos less prominently than your own company or product name
or logo." and "Do not modify the permitted GitHub logos, including changing the color, dimensions, or combining with other
words or design elements." The same page says that, beginning in 2025, GitHub Copilot no longer has a standalone logo that
heros the Copilot icon, so the logo shown is GitHub's.

What this site does: the Invertocat in white (the page is dark), unmodified, at a size smaller than the name of this
product, linked to https://github.com. The words "Copilot CLI" are the name of the host, in a separate element beside the
mark: the two are not drawn as one lockup and the mark does not say that mnema is GitHub's.

## The hosts shown by name

Every other host the product's README lists is shown by its name, and this folder holds no file of theirs. A host whose
vendor has not been read for a rule of use falls to its name: a logo is added here, with its source and its rule, or not
at all.
